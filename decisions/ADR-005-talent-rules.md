# ADR-005: Lead = top-billed actor; Director = exact credit; talent needs 3+ films in 2+ contexts

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Decision owner:** Yaw Omari Asiamah

## Context

Kwame's negotiators want directors and lead actors who reliably signal a strong title. Franchises reuse the same people: 13 of 66 directors and 19 of 48 leads have 3 or more films, often within one series. The thresholds were set from film counts only, before any box-office result for talent was viewed.

## Decision

The lead is the top-billed actor (lowest billing, then lowest person id). Directors are crew credited exactly as "Director". A person qualifies with 3 or more films across 2 or more contexts (a collection or a standalone counts as one context each), measured against the typical film in the same period, with the share of their films above it shown.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A: The top three billed actors count as leads

- **How it would work:** one setting changes from 0 to 2.
- **Why it is attractive:** ensemble films have several leads.
- **Why rejected:** top billing is the standard signal; not tested before the deadline.
- **Recommendation under it:** not tested; the franchise recommendation cannot move (it does not use talent).

### Alternative B: Count Co-Director credits as directors

- **How it would work:** add "Co-Director" to the director jobs setting.
- **Why it is attractive:** credits 7 more people.
- **Why rejected:** a separate credit, not the lever the brief names.
- **Recommendation under it:** only Lee Unkrich (2 credits) is near the threshold; no qualifying name changes.

### Alternative C: 2 films are enough

- **How it would work:** lower the minimum.
- **Why it is attractive:** more names for negotiators.
- **Why rejected:** one franchise sequel pair would qualify.
- **Recommendation under it:** more names, mostly franchise pairs.

## Consequences

- **What this buys:** a talent read that separates people from franchises.
- **What this costs:** on 112 films, almost nobody qualifies outside one series; only Christopher Nolan does, and three of his films are on the shortlist.
- **What it costs the client if I am wrong:** negotiators could press a talent point the evidence does not support.
- **What would make me revisit this:** a longer list with more standalones, or connected series (Iron Man and The Avengers) grouped in the data.

## What this survives

A 500-title list: the rules apply unchanged and more people may qualify; shared film universes still need a human check.
